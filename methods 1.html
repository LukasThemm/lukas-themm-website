Integrational Methods

How I bring meditation, movement, and strength training into creative production

Creative projects ask different things of me at different stages. When I’m developing a concept, I need room to explore ideas. Once production begins, I need to make clear decisions, keep my commitments, and adapt when plans change. My personal practice brings together meditation, tai chi, yoga, breathwork, and strength training to support both stages.

Meditation helps me focus my attention. Tai chi and yoga help me notice how I move and where I hold tension. Before a demanding conversation, I use breathwork to pause and collect my thoughts. Strength training reminds me how much progress comes from showing up consistently.

From creative concept to project delivery

As a creative producer, I bring these habits into the way I work with people. When developing a concept, I give myself time to test an idea before committing to it. In team conversations, I listen carefully and clarify who can contribute what. If plans shift during production, I reset priorities and agree on the next step with the team.

My aim is to communicate an idea clearly enough for others to help shape it, then work together to bring it to life.
