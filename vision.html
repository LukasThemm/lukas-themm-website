Vision & Philosophy

Vision

Let There Be Light. I want to create once-in-a-lifetime experiences for visitors. Light can change how we feel in a space, draw strangers into a shared moment, and send them home with a little of that wonder to pass on.

Philosophy

I follow what feels true to my heart, stay connected with others, and offer support where I can. For me, mastery of body and mind is a daily practice of movement, focus, and attention. When I stay present, I give wonder room to unfold.
