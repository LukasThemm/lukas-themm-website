Vision & Philosophy

Vision

Let There Be Light

I want to create once-in-a-lifetime experiences for visitors. Light - understood beyond the physicals - can change how we feel in a space and effect people with a little of that wonder to pass on. Sending them home with such a good vibe, which meant to be shared as a multiplicator of love. Reminding people of the beauty of life and bringing wonders alive!

Philosophy

I follow what feels true to my heart, stay connected with others, and offer support where I can. For me, mastery of body and mind is a daily practice of movement, focus, and attention. When I stay present, I give wonder room to unfold.
